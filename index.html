The experience / one connected day

A first pass.
A new perspective.

You do not need to know the offside rule. Start with a simple pass, meet a welcoming host, and discover the match together. The proposed day brings family participation and a real club fixture into one experience.

01 / PLAY

Learn by doing.

60 minutes of beginner parent-and-child skills and cooperative games in a separate safe activity area. Share small successes, without pressure to be an expert.

02 / CONNECT

Share the story.

Enjoy a simple lunch, hear a club-approved story and ask the host about watching football. Get to know the club before cheering it on.

03 / WATCH

Find your team.

Watch an actual Kingsbridge United home fixture in an approved viewing area, with a host nearby for questions. Then travel back to Exeter together.

Good to know / before your first visit

A few first questions.

The details matter. These answers describe the proposed offer and what must be confirmed before families can book.

Do we need football experience?

No. The proposed session is designed for beginners. A parent or accompanying adult participates with the child, and a host introduces the match. You do not need to support a club already.

What does the proposed £49 include?

One adult and one child aged 6–11: return coach from Exeter, a 60-minute beginner session, simple lunch, a club story and rules guide, and admission to the home fixture. The proposal also includes an optional practice card and follow-up. Additional adults or children cannot be added to a family unit without a revised capacity and price.

Where and when will we travel?

The proposed trip starts with one Exeter pickup and travels to Kingsbridge United in Devon, returning after the home match. The exact pickup, date, departure and return times depend on the confirmed fixture and coach operator. They will be published before paid booking opens.

Is language or access support available?

Please ask. English is welcome, and Chinese heritage does not imply a particular language. Requested language, access and dietary support will be discussed and confirmed individually before booking. No particular support service is guaranteed at this planning stage.

How do supported places work?

Four family units at £20 each are proposed through a confidential, need-based process. Details and availability will be explained when the event is approved. There is no application or payment collection on this prototype.

Who looks after children, and what if plans change?

Adults remain with their own children throughout. Club-approved welfare, coaching and safe viewing arrangements must be confirmed before launch. Photography is optional. The club will confirm weather, fixture-change and cancellation arrangements, including refund or agreed rescheduling terms, before taking payments. No player meeting or access to the main match pitch is promised.
